::: zh
编曲与作曲的界线不在工作量，而在改动的性质。作曲决定音高关系、节奏骨架与段落比例，编曲决定这些材料由谁、在哪个音区、以什么织体呈现。把两者分开，才能知道哪些改动会改变作品身份。

## 问题
把所有声部都当成不可动的素材，编曲就只剩配器。反过来，改掉旋律走向或和声骨架却仍叫编曲，会让合作方误解改动范围。

## 织体或频段
作曲层是旋律轮廓、低音走向、和声功能与时间比例。编曲层是音色、音区、层数、节奏型与段落的进出。判断时可问一句，换掉乐器与织体后，主题还认得出吗。认得出，改动主要在编曲。

## 做法
先列两张清单。素材清单写下旋律、低音、和声与节奏型，标出不可改的核心。实现清单写下音色、音区、织体与段落安排。改编时先保留骨架，再换载体。若改到骨架，就把它当作共同作曲，而不是编曲。

## 听什么
对照巴赫管风琴原版与钢琴改编，听脚踏板低音怎样移进左手。再听《牛虻》西班牙舞曲的钢琴版，注意原管弦色彩被哪些音区替代。

## 常见误解
编曲不等于配器。改调、改拍也可能触及作曲。界线按作品身份是否改变来判，不按写下的音符数量来判。

::: en
The line between composition and arrangement is not workload but the nature of the change. Composition fixes pitch relationships, rhythmic skeleton and proportions. Arrangement decides who carries that material, in which register and with what texture. Separating the two tells you which edits alter the identity of a piece.

## The problem
Treat every part as untouchable and arranging shrinks to orchestration. Change the melodic contour or harmonic skeleton while still calling it arranging, and collaborators misread the scope.

## Texture or frequency band
The compositional layer is melody contour, bass motion, harmonic function and timing. The arranging layer is timbre, register, layer count, rhythmic pattern and section entries. Ask whether the theme remains recognisable after instruments and texture are replaced. If it does, the work is mainly arranging.

## Approach
Write two lists. The material list records melody, bass, harmony and rhythmic pattern and marks what cannot change. The realisation list records timbre, register, texture and section plan. Keep the skeleton, then change the vehicle. If the skeleton itself changes, treat it as co-writing rather than arranging.

## What to listen for
Compare Bach's organ original with the piano transcription and hear the pedal line move into the left hand. Then hear the piano version of the Gadfly Spanish Dance and notice which registers replace the orchestral colours.

## Common misunderstandings
Arranging is not orchestration. Transposition or metre change may also touch composition. Judge by whether identity changes, not by note count.
