## 问题

铜管的音量与音色随力度变化极大。同一支圆号，弱奏时能融进木管，强奏时能压过整个弦乐组。写作首先要决定用哪一档力度。

## 乐器组合

圆号柔和，最易与木管、弦乐融合；小号明亮，负责信号与高点；长号庄严，也能做滑音；大号守住低音。四支一组时，音区要拉开，避免全部挤在中高音。铜管需要休息，连续高音会让演奏者失去控制。

## 音区与掩蔽

铜管齐奏的能量集中在中高音，最容易盖住木管。圆号放在中音区，可以与巴松、大提琴交叠；小号放在高音区，容易成为前景。写作时给铜管留出空拍，比让它一直吹更有效。

## 听什么

《普通人的号角》里铜管与定音鼓的对话，听齐奏的重量；《贵族行列》的四支小号改编，听同一家族内部的音区分配；维尔纳《第一交响曲》改编版的铜管与管风琴，听铜管怎样在巨大背景中被听见。

## 常见误解

以为铜管越响越有气势。真正有说服力的是短句、弱奏与准确的进入；一直强奏只会让耳朵疲劳。

## The problem

Brass changes volume and colour enormously with dynamics. The same horn can blend into woodwind at a soft level and overpower a whole string section at a loud one. The first decision in brass writing is which dynamic level the music needs.

## Instrument combination

The horn is soft and blends most easily with woodwind and strings. The trumpet is bright and carries signals and peaks. The trombone is solemn and can slide. The tuba holds the bass. In a four-part group, spread the registers rather than crowding the middle-high range. Brass needs rest; unbroken high playing costs players control.

## Register and masking

Brass tutti energy sits in the middle-high range and easily covers woodwind. A horn in the middle register can overlap bassoon and cello; a trumpet high up becomes foreground. Leaving rests for the brass is more effective than keeping it playing throughout.

## What to listen for

In Fanfare for the Common Man, hear the dialogue between brass and timpani and the weight of the tutti. In the four-trumpet arrangement of Procession of the Nobles, hear how one family divides its registers. In the orchestral arrangement of Vierne's First Symphony, hear brass make itself heard against organ and strings.

## Common misconceptions

The belief that louder brass is more impressive misses the point. Short phrases, soft playing and precise entries carry far more authority; constant fortissimo only tires the ear.
