::: zh
铜管写四部和声，最贵的不是音符，是气。一个高音写在错误的位置，整段排练都要重来。

## 先算气，再算音

铜管的耐力按分钟掉。小号在极高音区连续吹八小节，后面整段都会发虚；圆号的长音看着省力，其实最耗嘴唇。写作时把高音留给短句，长音放在中音区，声部之间轮流休息。整段齐奏在谱面上很威风，录到第三遍就散了。

## 四部怎么落

圆号负责中间，小号在上，长号铺中低，大号托底。这不是固定公式，但顺序有理由：圆号的音色能融进木管与弦乐，放在内声部不容易突出来；小号在高音区最有效率，低音区反而单薄；长号强奏时声音宽，写密集和声会糊；大号最好只拿根音，别让它跑旋律。

| 声部 | 常用音区 | 角色 | 常见错误 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 小号 | C4 到 C6 | 顶声部、信号 | 长音留在高音区 |
| 圆号 | C3 到 C5 | 内声部、融合 | 与长号写同度 |
| 长号 | C3 到 F4 | 中低和声 | 低音区写三度叠置 |
| 大号 | C1 到 C3 | 根音、低音 | 写快速旋律 |

表内音区按实际发声音高记。

## 强奏、弱奏与弱音器

强奏时铜管整体容易偏高，三度在低音区容易打架，用八度与五度更稳。弱奏时不要把四支都留在低音区，声音会闷成一团；让圆号或小号带一句，其他声部让开。弱音器改变的不只是音色，还有平衡：加弱音器的小号会更靠前，圆号则退得更远。[[glossary:voicing|声部排列]]要和力度一起想，不能先排和弦再补力度。

## 哪里会坏

四支都写高音、都写长音、都在强奏，结果是谁也听不清。低音区把[[glossary:spacing|间距]]挤得太紧，会变成一片轰鸣；大号写快速音型，音头跟不上。连续演奏超过几分钟，最后一遍的质量通常会低于第一遍；改编时把弦乐的长音原样搬给铜管，往往撑不住。采样铜管的渐强常常只是音量曲线，写太长的渐强会露出接缝。

::: en
The most expensive thing in a four-part brass chord is not the notes; it is the air. Put one high note in the wrong place and the whole rehearsal starts again.

## Count the breath first

Brass stamina falls by the minute. Eight bars in the extreme high register leave a trumpet weak for the rest of the page, and a long horn note that looks easy costs the most lip. Write high notes in short phrases, keep sustained notes in the middle, and let the parts rest in turn. A continuous tutti looks impressive on paper and falls apart on the third take.

## Placing four parts

Horns take the middle, trumpets the top, trombones the middle-low, and the tuba the floor. It is not a fixed rule, but the order has reasons: the horn blends into woodwind and strings, so an inner part does not stick out; the trumpet is most efficient high up and thin low down; trombones spread wide at loud dynamics, so dense harmony turns muddy; the tuba is better on roots than on melody.

| Part | Register | Role | Common fault |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trumpet | C4 to C6 | Top line, signal | Sustains left high |
| Horn | C3 to C5 | Inner blend | Unison with trombone |
| Trombone | C3 to F4 | Middle-low harmony | Thirds stacked low |
| Tuba | C1 to C3 | Root and bass | Fast melodic writing |

## Loud, soft, muted

At high dynamics brass tends to go sharp, and thirds in the low register fight; octaves and fifths hold better. At low dynamics, do not leave all four parts in the basement or the sound turns into a mutter. Let a horn or trumpet lead a phrase while the others step back. Mutes change balance as well as colour: a muted trumpet moves forward, a muted horn moves back. Think about voicing and dynamics together rather than adding markings afterwards.

## Where it breaks

Four parts all high, all sustained and all loud means nobody is heard. Pack the low register too tightly and it becomes a roar; give the tuba a fast figure and the attack lags. After several minutes of continuous playing, the last take is usually weaker than the first; lifting string sustains straight into the brass often does not survive. Sampled brass crescendos are often just volume curves, and a long one exposes the seam.
