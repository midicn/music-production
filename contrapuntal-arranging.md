::: zh
对位式编曲不是把两条旋律叠在一起，而是让每条线单独成立，合起来又能听出和声。它适合段落需要智力感，或需要把两个主题并置的时候。

## 问题
两条线都写得很满，听者只会听到节奏，不会听到对位。先问每条线拿掉对方后还能不能唱，再决定叠不叠。

## 织体或频段
两条线最好分居不同音区，节奏错开，一条动时另一条静，一条上行时另一条下行。三声部以上时，中声部最容易糊，通常让它做长音或模仿的尾句。音区要留出辨认的缝隙，不必刻意分频段。

## 做法与判断
先写一条完整的主线，再写一条能单独唱的反向或斜向线条。用模仿、倒影、扩增制造关系，但每四小节要有一个清楚的终止或呼吸。若两条线都做密集切分，先删掉一条的装饰音。

## 听什么
听赋格的主题进入，每次进入都在不同声部与音区。再听四重奏的末乐章，注意大提琴的固定音型如何与高音旋律形成两条独立线索。

## 常见误解
对位不等于复调堆叠。两条线如果离开对方就不能成立，那只是和声的分层。

::: en
Contrapuntal arranging does not mean stacking two melodies. It means making each line stand on its own while the combination still reveals harmony. It suits a passage that needs intellectual bite.

## The problem
If both lines are full, the listener hears rhythm rather than counterpoint. Ask whether each line can be sung without the other before combining them.

## Texture or frequency band
Place the lines in different registers and offset their rhythms, one moving while the other rests, one rising while the other falls. With three or more parts, the middle is most likely to blur, so give it long notes or the tail of an imitation. Leave a register gap for recognition.

## Approach and judgement
Write one complete main line, then a contrary or oblique line that can be sung alone. Build relationships through imitation, inversion and augmentation, but provide a clear cadence every four bars. If both lines are densely syncopated, remove the ornaments from one first.

## What to listen for
In a fugue, follow the subject as it enters in a different voice and register each time. In a quartet finale, notice how a repeated cello figure and the upper melody form two separate threads.

## Common misunderstandings
Counterpoint is not piled-up polyphony. If two lines cannot stand without each other, they are only harmonic layers.
