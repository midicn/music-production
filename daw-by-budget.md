::: zh
预算不是一次性支出。软件只是第一笔，后面还有升级、音源、插件与云服务。把三年放在一张账上，免费、教育、订阅与买断的差别才看得清。

## 要判断什么

先分清一次性支出与持续支出。再看授权限制：教育版能否商用、能否升级、毕业或离职后是否失效。最后看时间成本：免费方案省下的钱，往往用学习与兼容性补回去。

## 选择表

零预算起步，用 MuseScore 写谱，用 GarageBand 或 Waveform Free 做音频，需要学后期时用 Pro Tools 的免费入门档；教育版用 Apple 教育套装里的 Logic Pro，或 Ableton Live、Cubase 的教育授权；长期订阅适合需要持续更新与行业交换的岗位，如 Pro Tools 与 Audition；买断适合三五年不换工具的固定流程，如 Cubase、Studio One、FL Studio、Bitwig Studio、REAPER 与 Dorico；按量适合游戏中间件，Wwise 与 FMOD 按项目规模分档。

## 三种情形

学生宿舍：Mac 上用 GarageBand 起步，需要谱面就加 MuseScore，一学期不必买任何东西。半职业接活：Logic Pro 或 Cubase 买断，插件先只用原厂，收入稳定后再补一套混响与一套修复。后期岗位：Pro Tools 的订阅是行业交换的门票，另配 Audition 或 RX 处理对白。

## 边界与坑

- 教育版通常限本人、限非商业，毕业或离职要重新授权。
- 免费档常限制轨道数、导出格式或插件格式，做到一半才发现。
- 订阅的总额在三年尺度上会超过买断，但买断的跨版本升级也要钱。
- 套装与捆绑容易买多，先用三个月再补。
- 价格与条款按地区与时间变，付款前按官方页面核对。

## 怎么开始

先列一张三年账：软件、升级、音源、插件、云服务。再用同一首短曲在免费方案里走完录音到导出，确认缺的到底是功能还是经验。读这里的常见 DAW 对比与插件格式两页。

::: en
A budget is not a single purchase. The software is the first payment; upgrades, sounds, plug-ins and cloud services follow. Put three years on one ledger and the difference between free, education, subscription and perpetual becomes visible.

## What you are deciding

Separate one-off spending from recurring spending. Then read the licence: whether an education copy allows commercial work, whether it upgrades, and what happens after graduation or leaving a job. Finally count time: money saved on a free option is often paid back in learning and compatibility.

## The decision table

With no budget, write scores in MuseScore, make audio in GarageBand or Waveform Free, and use the free entry tier of Pro Tools when post work has to be learned. For education, take Logic Pro through an Apple education bundle, or the education licences of Ableton Live and Cubase. Long subscriptions suit jobs that need constant updates and industry exchange, such as Pro Tools and Audition. Perpetual licences suit a fixed workflow kept for years, such as Cubase, Studio One, FL Studio, Bitwig Studio, REAPER and Dorico. Usage-based terms suit game middleware, where Wwise and FMOD tier by project scale.

## Three cases

A student room: start with GarageBand on a Mac, add MuseScore for scores, and buy nothing for a semester. A semi-pro freelancer: buy Logic Pro or Cubase outright, use stock plug-ins first, and add one reverb and one repair tool once income is steady. A post job: a Pro Tools subscription is the entry ticket to exchange, with Audition or RX handling dialogue.

## Limits and traps

- Education licences are usually personal and non-commercial, and must be replaced after graduation or leaving.
- Free tiers often cap track count, export formats or plug-in formats, which you discover halfway through.
- Over three years a subscription usually costs more than a perpetual licence, but perpetual licences charge for major upgrades.
- Bundles invite overbuying; wait three months before adding anything.
- Prices and terms shift by region and time; check the official page before paying.

## How to start

Write a three-year ledger: software, upgrades, sounds, plug-ins, cloud. Then finish one short piece entirely inside the free option, and decide whether what was missing was a feature or experience. Read Comparing DAWs and Plug-in Formats alongside.
