纪录片里的音乐经常被采访打断。一段音乐要能在人声进来时退到背景，在人声停下时又把空间接住，这比写一段好听的旋律更难。

## 采访先于音乐

先做 spotting，但标的是采访的句读、环境声的缝隙与资料的年代，而不是戏剧转折。旁白、同期声与环境声的优先顺序要写在同一张 spotting 表上，剪辑、混音与作曲看的必须是同一版。采访段落的音乐通常要压到比对白低很多的位置，或者用侧链让音乐随人声下沉。更稳妥的做法是编曲时就留出中频：不要让持续的和弦铺满 1 到 4 kHz，也不要用带歌词的人声。

## 长段铺底怎么写

长段铺底靠三件事成立：一个可重复的音型、缓慢的和声变化、以及不抢画面的音色。音型不必复杂，但要能持续五到十分钟不烦；和声变化要慢到观众注意不到切换；音色避开尖锐的高频与强烈的低频脉冲。铺底的长度按场景算，不按乐句算；镜头切走时要能随时退出。历史题材可以用当时的乐器或录音质感，但不要做成模仿秀——质感服务叙述，不是主角。

| 场景 | 音乐要做什么 | 常见做法 |

| 采访 | 让字清楚 | 中频让位、侧链下沉、无歌词 |

## 边界：别替画面下结论

音乐不能替采访者下结论。若画面还在陈述事实，音乐已经给出判决，观众会觉得被操纵。也不要让铺底从头铺到尾：没有静默，采访的停顿就没有重量。素材来源不清的档案音乐、需要额外授权的唱片片段，都会在发行时变成问题。纪录片配乐的第一标准是可信，而不是煽情。

::: en
Documentary music is interrupted by interviews. A cue has to step back when a voice enters and hold the space when the voice stops, which is harder than writing a pleasant tune.

## Interview first

Do the spotting, but mark the sentence rhythm of interviews, the gaps in location sound and the period of the archive, not dramatic turns. Narration, sync sound and ambience need a priority order on the same spotting sheet, and editing, mixing and composition must read the same version. Music under an interview usually sits far below dialogue, or is ducked by a sidechain. The safer method is to leave the midrange open in the arrangement: no sustained chord filling one to four kilohertz, no voice with lyrics.

## Writing a long bed

Three things make a long bed work: a repeatable figure, slow harmonic change and a timbre that does not compete with the image. The figure can be simple but must survive five to ten minutes without irritation. Harmony changes slowly enough that the listener misses the switch. The timbre avoids sharp highs and strong low pulses. Measure the bed by scene rather than by phrase, so it can leave the moment the shot changes. Period subjects can use period instruments or recording texture, but not as an impersonation; the texture serves the narration.

## Four scene types

| Scene | What the music does | Common approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview | Keeps words clear | Clear the midrange, duck, no lyrics |
| Archive | Establishes a period | Light noise floor, period instruments, narrow band |
| Empty shot / transition | Holds time | Long tones, fixed figure, few chord changes |
| Emotional turn | Offers a judgement | Thicken once at the turn, not continuously |

## The boundary

Music must not reach a verdict for the interviewee. If the image is still stating facts and the score has already judged, the audience feels manipulated. Nor should a bed run from beginning to end: without silence, an interview pause has no weight. Archive music with unclear provenance and licensed record excerpts become clearance problems at release. Documentary scoring is judged by credibility before emotion.
