## 能替你做完什么

做一版好莱坞式的管弦草稿，铜管与打击乐足够撑住预告片。用 Symphonic Orchestra 补上木管与竖琴的色彩。用 Hollywood Choirs 的 WordBuilder 让人声唱出可听的词。用 Stormdrum 铺电影打击乐。它常被用来写游戏 Boss 战的整段音乐，也适合做需要大鼓与铜管的片头。从 ComposerCloud 取整套音色，按项目换用。把分轨交出去，或只保留订阅期内的成品音频。

## 它怎么工作

采样按乐器与演奏法组织，OPUS 引擎流式读取，授权走 iLok。OPUS 可以按麦克风组预载，把当前 cue 不用的采样移出内存；同一套 keyswitch 在不同库之间尽量保持一致。录音在 EastWest 自己的 scoring stage，厅堂感明显，近场麦可以拉回一些细节。演奏法用 keyswitch 与 mod wheel 切换与控制，连奏与快速经过句有专门 patch。订阅版按月付费，买断版按产品单独授权。

## 哪里会坑你

第一，iLok 是硬门槛：没有 iLok 账户就不能用，机器授权与加密狗二选一，换机要迁。第二，OPUS 吃 CPU 也吃加载时间，低缓冲、多麦克风时容易爆音，项目打开要等。第三，体积接近 TB 级，Gold 版是它的缩减，SSD 与备份盘都要留。第四，录音带明显舞台感，想贴耳要大量 close 麦与 EQ，厅堂尾巴长，密集配器容易糊，独奏乐器的真实度不如专门的独奏库。第五，订阅停付就失去访问，买断不可转售，工程里只剩渲染好的音频。第六，WordBuilder 要学发音拼写，英语之外的语言更费事。第七，团队协作要给每台工作机安排授权，不能靠拷贝工程解决。

## 换成什么

要更干、更可控的厅，换 Orchestral Tools；要更湿更成套的英国厅，换 Spitfire；只要合唱，单独买合唱库更省；做纯预告片音效，Heavyocity 的打击与纹理更直接。它常与 Omnisphere、Stormdrum 叠 hybrid 层，用限制器把预告片的冲击做出来。

## 先学三件事

先学 OPUS 的麦克风与预载、keyswitch 与 mod wheel、ComposerCloud 的安装管理。不要一上来就装全套，先在一段 30 秒的 cue 里跑通全流程，再读这里的管弦乐配器、合唱写作、采样库整理三篇。

## What it finishes for you

A Hollywood-style orchestral sketch, with brass and percussion strong enough for a trailer. Symphonic Orchestra adds woodwind and harp colour. Hollywood Choirs and WordBuilder let a choir sing intelligible words. Stormdrum supplies cinematic percussion. The library suits a whole boss-battle cue or a title sequence with big drums and brass. ComposerCloud provides the whole catalogue to switch between projects. Stems can be delivered, or only the rendered audio survives beyond a subscription.

## How it works

Samples are organised by instrument and articulation, streamed by the OPUS engine, and authorised through iLok. OPUS preloads by microphone group, so samples a cue does not need leave memory, and keyswitch layouts stay as consistent as possible between libraries. Recording happens on EastWest's own scoring stage, so the room is obvious and close mics recover some detail. Articulations switch by keyswitch, dynamics follow the mod wheel, and dedicated patches cover legato and fast runs. Subscriptions are monthly; perpetual licences are sold per product.

## Where it bites

iLok is a hard gate: without an account nothing runs, and a machine licence or a dongle must be chosen and migrated when hardware changes. OPUS is heavy on CPU and on load time, and low buffers with many microphones crackle, while opening a project means waiting. The library approaches a terabyte, Gold is the reduced version, and both an SSD and a backup drive are needed. The stage sound is strong, so an intimate mix takes close mics and EQ, long hall tails make dense writing muddy, and solo instruments are less convincing than a dedicated solo library. Stop paying and access ends; perpetual licences cannot be resold, leaving only rendered audio in old projects. WordBuilder also takes practice, and languages other than English take more. Team work needs a licence seat per machine; copying a project does not help.

## What to swap in

Orchestral Tools gives a drier, more controllable room; Spitfire offers a wetter British hall; if only choir is needed, a dedicated choir library is cheaper; for pure trailer sound design, Heavyocity's percussion and textures are more direct. It layers well with Omnisphere and Stormdrum, with a limiter for trailer impact.

## First three things

Learn OPUS microphone choices and preload, keyswitch and mod-wheel control, and how ComposerCloud installs are managed. Do not install the whole catalogue first; get one thirty-second cue through the full chain, then read the site's orchestration, choir scoring, and library organisation entries.
