电子编曲从循环与音色开始。鼓、贝斯、主音与铺底各占一层，先用八小节立住骨架，再决定哪里加、哪里删。它的段落感更多来自滤波、静音与低频进出，而不是写更多音符。

## 问题
循环层层叠加，每段听起来一样。低频与鼓抢同一块空间，主音色反而被埋住。

## 织体或频段
按频段分工。次低频给底鼓与超低音，中低频给和弦与琶音，中高频给主音与人声，高频给镲片与空气。低频保持单声道，宽度留给中高频。

## 做法
先做八小节循环，只留鼓、贝斯与一个主音色。每八小节换一层，加铺底、换滤波或加打击。副歌前用一两小节降层或降频，进入时只加回低频与鼓。用自动化写滤波与音量，不要只靠静音开关。

## 听什么
听迷幻 trance 循环里方波与锯齿主音怎样叠加。听 quantum 循环里鼓、锯齿合成器与贝斯的分工。再听流行鼓循环，注意底鼓与军鼓怎样先定骨架。

## 常见误解
电子编曲不等于音色堆叠，删层与滤波更能制造段落。低音也不是越宽越好，单声道更稳。

::: en
Electronic arranging starts with loops and timbre. Drums, bass, lead and pad each occupy a layer. Build an eight-bar skeleton first, then decide what to add or remove. Sections come more from filtering, muting and low-end entries than from writing extra notes.

## The problem
Loops stack up and every section sounds the same. The bass and kick fight for the same space, and the lead disappears underneath.

## Texture or frequency band
Assign frequency ranges. Sub-bass goes to the kick and sub, low-mid to chords and arpeggios, upper-mid to lead and voice, highs to hats and air. Keep the low end mono and leave width for the upper range.

## Approach
Make an eight-bar loop with only drums, bass and one lead. Change one layer every eight bars by adding a pad, moving a filter or adding percussion. Drop a layer or filter the low end for one or two bars before a chorus, then bring back only bass and drums. Automate filter and level rather than relying on mute switches.

## What to listen for
Hear the square and saw leads stack in a trance loop. Hear the drums, saw synth and bass divide their roles in the quantum loop. Then hear a pop drum loop and notice how kick and snare set the skeleton first.

## Common misunderstandings
Electronic arranging is not stacking timbres, since muting and filtering create sections more clearly. A wider bass is not always better, and mono is steadier.
