::: zh
情绪曲线是张力与回落的安排，不是一路渐强。先定起点、转折与落点，再决定每段的能量。最高点不一定要最响，最高音、最密织体或最长的延迟解决都能成为顶点。

## 问题
每段都用力，曲线会变直线。高潮来得太早，后面只能重复，结尾也收不住。

## 织体或频段
用三条线画曲线。音区负责明亮与压迫，和声张力负责期待，节奏密度负责紧迫。低频与长音负责稳定，高频与密集音型负责收紧。顶点之后要让至少一条线先回落。

## 做法
标出全曲三个节点，起点、转折、落点。把最高音或最大密度放在三分之二处，最后三分之一做减法。若结尾收不住，先删层，不要只降速。段落之间用一次明确的解决或停顿分隔。

## 听什么
听肖邦第一叙事曲的多次起伏与回落。听贝多芬 Op.111 末乐章变奏怎样逐步稀释。再听《特里斯坦》选段，注意和声张力被延迟多久才解决。

## 常见误解
情绪曲线不等于渐强，回落与停顿才是关键。最高点也不一定最响，可能只是最空或最高。

::: en
An emotional arc is an arrangement of tension and release, not an unbroken crescendo. Fix the starting point, the turn and the landing, then decide the energy of each section. The peak need not be the loudest moment. A highest note, a densest texture or a long delayed resolution can all serve as the summit.

## The problem
When every section pushes, the curve flattens. A climax that arrives too early leaves only repetition, and the ending cannot close.

## Texture or frequency band
Draw the curve with three lines. Register carries brightness and pressure, harmonic tension carries expectation, rhythmic density carries urgency. Low end and long notes provide stability, while highs and busy figures tighten. After the peak, let at least one line fall first.

## Approach
Mark three nodes, the start, the turn and the landing. Place the highest note or the densest passage around two thirds of the way through, then subtract during the final third. If the ending will not close, remove layers before slowing the tempo. Separate sections with one clear resolution or pause.

## What to listen for
Hear the repeated rises and falls in Chopin's First Ballade. Hear the variations thin out in the finale of Beethoven's Op. 111. In the Tristan excerpt, notice how long the harmonic tension is delayed before it resolves.

## Common misunderstandings
An emotional arc is not a crescendo, since the falls and pauses make it work. The peak need not be the loudest point either, and may be the sparsest or highest one.
