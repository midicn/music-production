## 常见卡点

音乐会总谱只对时间负责，影视总谱要对画面负责。指挥和录音师需要知道小节与画面的对应关系，需要看到每条提示线、每个音效点，还要能在录音棚里跟着时间码走。排版不漂亮可以接受，时间对不上不行。

## 原因

影视配乐通常按时间码写成段落，每段从某个镜头或事件起拍。总谱上标出时间码、节拍器标记与画面提示线，方便指挥在录音时对准。音乐常常需要变速、停顿或延长来贴合剪辑，因此谱面上会出现自由节拍、延长记号与临时拍号。总谱之外还有分谱，分谱只保留该声部与必要的提示，录音时用。

## 怎么绕过去

先确认交付版本的时间码与帧率。给每段标清起止与小节号，方便剪辑师回找。在关键画面处写提示线，不要每帧都标。变速段用明确的节拍变化标记，别只写自由速度。总谱与分谱分开导出，分谱要带上排练号与提示音符。录音时留出对画面的余量，剪辑改动后要能快速改谱。

## 听这几处

在弦乐四重奏的慢乐章里听乐句与画面情绪的对应，在持续低音里听悬疑铺陈的层次，在短动机的推进里听长镜头的张力。配乐是否成立，最终看音乐与画面是否互相成就。

## Common snags

A concert score answers to time alone, while a film score answers to picture. The conductor and recording engineer must see how bars line up with shots, where every cue line and sound event sits, and how to follow timecode in the studio. Plain engraving is acceptable; a score that does not line up is not.

## The reason

Film scores are usually written in cues, each beginning at a shot or event. The score marks timecode, metronome values and picture cue lines so the conductor can hit them during recording. Music often needs to stretch, pause or extend to fit the cut, which puts free metre, fermatas and changing signatures on the page. Separate parts are extracted for recording, keeping only the player's line plus necessary cues.

## How to get around it

Confirm the delivery timecode and frame rate first. Mark start and end plus bar numbers for every cue so editors can find them again. Draw cue lines at key picture moments, not on every frame. Notate tempo changes explicitly in stretching passages instead of writing free time. Export score and parts separately, with rehearsal marks and cue notes in the parts. Leave room for picture changes, and keep the score easy to revise when the cut moves.

## Listen to these

In the quartet's slow movement, how phrases match the screen's emotional beat; in the held bass, how suspense builds in layers; in the short motivic drive, how tension holds across a long take. A score succeeds when music and picture carry each other.
