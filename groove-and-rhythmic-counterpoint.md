::: zh
律动来自各节奏层的互补，不是某一轨有多复杂。底鼓、贝斯、军鼓与踩镲各管一个时间位置，错开落点，音乐才会向前走。节奏对位就是让这些层各有身份，又能咬合。

## 问题
所有声部按同一节奏切分，听起来像一条加厚的旋律。层数越多，律动反而越死。

## 织体或频段
低频负责落点，中频负责切分，高频负责细分。三层不要同时落在同一格。三加三加二与六八对四四的交叉能产生推力，但至少保留一个稳定锚点。

## 做法
先写两小节的锚，通常是底鼓与低音。再让其他层错开半拍或一拍，用休止制造回应。若律动散，先删掉一层切分，保留一个正拍。加花时不要改变锚的位置。

## 听什么
听放克鼓循环里底鼓与军鼓的错位。听《枫叶拉格》左手二拍低音与右手切分的对位。再听《哈巴涅拉》，注意低音的三加三加二。

## 常见误解
律动不等于复杂，稳定的锚加一处错位就够了。节奏对位也不是复调，重点在时间上的互补。

::: en
Groove comes from rhythmic layers completing one another, not from one complicated part. Kick, bass, snare and hats each own a moment in time, and the music moves because their accents fall apart. Rhythmic counterpoint gives each layer an identity while keeping them interlocked.

## The problem
When every part syncopates in the same way, the result sounds like one thickened melody. More layers make the groove stiffer.

## Texture or frequency band
Low end owns the landing points, the middle owns syncopation, and the top owns subdivision. Do not let all three land in the same grid cell. Three-plus-three-plus-two and six-eight against four-four can create drive, but keep at least one steady anchor.

## Approach
Write a two-bar anchor first, usually kick and bass. Offset the other layers by a half or full beat and use rests to create answers. If the groove scatters, remove one syncopated layer and keep a downbeat. Do not move the anchor when adding a fill.

## What to listen for
Hear the kick and snare offset in a funk loop. Hear the two-beat left hand against the syncopated right hand in Maple Leaf Rag. Then hear the three-plus-three-plus-two bass in the Habanera.

## Common misunderstandings
Groove is not complexity, since one steady anchor plus one offset is often enough. Rhythmic counterpoint is not counterpoint in the contrapuntal sense either, since the point is complementing in time.
