::: zh
和声铺底的任务不是把和弦弹出来，而是让旋律有一个稳定背景，同时不抢旋律的位置。铺得太满，旋律会失去轮廓，铺得太薄，段落会没有支撑。

## 问题
铺底与旋律同时换音，听者会分不清主次。先决定铺底在哪一拍静止，比决定加什么音色更重要。

## 织体或频段
铺底常放在中音区，用长音、分解音型或缓慢的声部移动。它要和旋律错开，旋律动时铺底静，旋律停时铺底可以稍微动一下。频段上避开人声最敏感的中高频，把能量留在中低与中频。

## 做法与判断
先决定每小节换一次还是每两小节换一次和弦。换得太快，铺底会变成节奏声部，换得太慢，和声会拖。用转位与声部连接减少跳进，让内声部尽量级进。若铺底与吉他或钢琴的伴奏重叠，留一件就够了。

## 听什么
听管风琴圣咏，长和弦之间靠低声部连接，旋律始终浮在最上面。再听弦乐慢乐章，内声部几乎不动，但和声颜色一直在变。

## 常见误解
铺底不等于 Pad 音色。弦乐、管风琴、人声长音都可以做铺底，关键在于时值与音区。

::: en
The job of a harmonic bed is not to state the chords but to give the melody a stable background without taking its place. Too full and the melody loses shape; too thin and the section loses support.

## The problem
When bed and melody change notes at the same moment, the ear cannot tell which is primary. Decide where the bed stands still before choosing the sound.

## Texture or frequency band
The bed usually sits in the middle register, as long notes, broken figures or slow voice movement. It should alternate with the melody, still while the melody moves and active when the melody pauses. Avoid the upper-mid range where voices are most sensitive.

## Approach and judgement
Choose whether chords change once per bar or once every two bars. Too fast and the bed becomes a rhythm part; too slow and the harmony drags. Use inversions and smooth voice leading to avoid leaps, keeping inner parts stepwise.

## What to listen for
In an organ chorale, long chords are joined by the lowest voice while the melody stays on top. In a slow string movement, the inner parts barely move although the harmonic colour keeps changing.

## Common misunderstandings
A bed is not a pad preset. Strings, organ and sustained voices can all provide one; what matters is duration and register.
