## 卡住的地方

混音师与乐手的职业伤害里，听力损失最容易被忽略，因为它发生得很慢。等到听不清高频、耳鸣持续不退，已经无法恢复。保护听力不是少干活，而是把音量与时长当成两个必须同时管的变量。

## 它怎么工作

耳朵里的毛细胞受损后不会再生。损伤取决于声压级与暴露时长的乘积：音量每增加约三分贝，安全时长减半。短期暴露后出现暂时性阈移，听感发闷，休息后能恢复；反复发生就会变成永久损失。低频不容易让人觉得吵，但能量大，同样会造成损伤。耳机把声源直接送进耳道，风险更高。

## 参数与做法

混音时把监听音量定在一个能连续工作数小时的水平，需要判断细节时短暂提高再降回。每工作四五十分钟休息几分钟，让耳朵恢复。排练与演出用降噪耳塞，选择频响较平的类型，避免只衰减高频的便宜耳塞。测一次自己的听音习惯，用声压计确认实际音量。出现耳鸣或发闷立即停止并休息，不要硬撑。

## 耳朵要抓的点

在灵魂乐鼓组的长排练里注意疲劳出现的时点，在室内乐的弱奏里注意自己是否在不知不觉地加大音量，在管风琴低频里注意耳机是否越听越响。听觉疲劳的第一个信号往往是"高频不见了"，而不是"声音太大"。

## Where it snags

Hearing loss is the most overlooked occupational injury among engineers and players because it arrives slowly. By the time high frequencies blur and tinnitus will not fade, nothing can be restored. Protecting hearing is not about working less but about treating level and duration as two variables that must be managed together.

## How it works

Hair cells in the ear do not regenerate. Damage depends on sound pressure level multiplied by exposure time: every extra three decibels roughly halves the safe duration. Short exposures cause a temporary threshold shift that feels dull and recovers with rest, but repeated shifts become permanent. Low frequencies do not feel loud yet carry great energy and damage just as well. Headphones deliver sound straight into the ear canal and raise the risk.

## Settings and practice

Set monitoring at a level you could work at for hours, and raise it only briefly when judging detail. Rest for a few minutes every forty to fifty minutes. Use flat-response earplugs for rehearsals and shows rather than cheap plugs that only cut highs. Measure your own habits with a sound level meter once. Stop and rest at the first sign of ringing or dullness instead of pushing through.

## What the ear catches

In a long soul rehearsal, note when fatigue arrives; in quiet chamber passages, whether you are creeping the volume up; in organ low end, whether the headphones get louder as you listen. The first sign of fatigue is usually missing high end, not a feeling that things are too loud.
