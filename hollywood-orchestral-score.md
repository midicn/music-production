::: zh
好莱坞交响配乐的核心不是编制大，而是用主题、和声与配器把情绪写得明确，并且在混录里给对白让路。

## 问题

大编制很容易盖住人声。中频被弦乐与铜管填满，对白就听不清；低频太厚，爆炸与脚步又失去空间。风格问题最终落回频率与动态的管理。

## 音画关系

主题通常放在高音弦乐或圆号，中频留给对白，低频交给大提琴、低音提琴与定音鼓。高潮靠铜管加厚与打击乐推进，安静段则撤掉中频，只留长音与高频。这样的安排让音乐可以在对白之下持续存在。

## 做法与判断

先定主题的音区，避免与人声重叠。铜管用在转折与揭示，不必在每个重音上都出现。判断时把对白轨打开，听主题是否还认得出；若认不出，先减配器而不是加音量。

## 听什么

听《天鹅湖》主题怎样由双簧管交给弦乐，听《舍赫拉查德》的铜管怎样在织体上再加一层，听《埃格蒙特》序曲从阴郁引子到凯旋尾声的跨度。演示用铜管叠一个和弦，注意中频有多快被填满。

## 常见误解

大编制不等于好配乐，室内乐编制同样能写出电影感。铜管也不是越响越壮，写得密反而糊。另一点，现代好莱坞配乐大量使用合成器与采样，交响只是外壳的一部分。

::: en
The point of a Hollywood orchestral score is not the size of the orchestra. It is the clarity of theme, harmony and scoring, plus the discipline to leave room for dialogue.

## The problem

A large orchestra covers the voice easily. When strings and brass fill the midrange, dialogue disappears; when the low end is thick, explosions and footsteps lose their space. Style comes back to frequency and dynamics.

## Music and picture

The theme usually sits in high strings or horns, the midrange is kept for dialogue, and the low end goes to cellos, basses and timpani. Brass and percussion push the climax; quiet passages withdraw from the midrange and leave long notes and top end. That arrangement lets the music continue underneath speech.

## Practice and judgement

Place the theme in a register that avoids the voice. Use brass for turns and reveals, not on every accent. Judge with the dialogue track open and ask whether the theme is still recognisable. If it is not, thin the scoring before raising the level.

## What to listen for

Hear the Swan Lake theme pass from oboe to strings, the brass add another layer in Scheherazade, and the Egmont overture travel from quiet ensemble to full tutti. The demo stacks a brass chord; notice how quickly the midrange fills.

## Common misunderstandings

A big orchestra does not guarantee a good score; chamber forces can sound cinematic. Brass is not more powerful when loud, and dense writing turns muddy. Modern scores also rely on synths and samples, with the orchestra as one layer.
