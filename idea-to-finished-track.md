::: zh
灵感要经过几个阶段才成为成品，每个阶段都要有「算完成」的标准，否则第八小节会一直改，歌却始终不存在。

## 问题

硬盘里躺着三十个八小节循环，每个都有希望，没有一个做完。每次开工都从音色设计开始，在结构之前结束，因为编曲是唯一不能靠更好的预设解决的问题。

## 做法

分阶段做。先在手机上把想法录下来，再开工程。用速度、调性与一个循环搭骨架。按目标长度编曲，定下引子、主歌、副歌与结尾的位置。替换临时音色，再混音，再母带。完成与判断之间隔一天。

## 参数与判断

开工前写下目标时长、参考曲与截止日。定一条规则，编曲不能从头放到尾之前，不做音色设计。每个阶段留一版粗混，后面的改动可以对比，而不是靠回忆。

## 听什么

用低音量与手机外放听粗混。段落仍然成立，说明结构有效。若离开音色就分不出主歌与副歌，对比是靠声音而不是靠音乐撑起来的。

## 常见误解

灵感不是完成度的引擎，截止日与关口才是。更多插件不会让曲子更强，完成也不等于完美。一首能结束的 demo，比一个完美的循环更值钱。

::: en
An idea becomes a track through stages, and each stage needs a definition of done. Otherwise the eighth bar keeps improving while the song never exists.

## The problem

The drive holds thirty loops of eight bars, all promising, none finished. Every session starts with sound design and ends before structure, because arrangement is the part that cannot be fixed by a better preset.

## The practice

Work in stages. Capture the idea on a phone before opening the DAW. Build a skeleton with tempo, key and one loop. Arrange to a target length, deciding where the intro, verse, chorus and ending sit. Replace placeholder sounds, then mix, then master. Put a day between finishing and judging.

## Settings and judgement

Write down target duration, a reference track and a deadline before starting. Set a rule that sound design stops until the arrangement runs from start to end. Keep a rough bounce at every stage, so a later change can be compared rather than remembered.

## What to listen for

Play the rough at low volume and on a phone speaker. If the sections still read, the structure works. If you cannot tell verse from chorus without the arrangement playing, the contrast is carried by sounds rather than by music.

## Common misunderstandings

Inspiration is not the engine of completion; deadlines and gates are. More plug-ins do not make a stronger track, and finishing does not mean perfect. A demo that ends is worth more than a perfect loop.
