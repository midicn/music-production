## 问题

两件乐器同时响，音量不会简单相加，音色也不会各自保留。组合要解决的是关系：谁在前景，谁在背景，谁负责把两者的缝隙填上。

## 乐器组合

三条常用准则：一强一弱、一高一低、一动一静。同族乐器音色接近，容易融合，却缺少对比；异族乐器对比鲜明，却要在音区与力度上让路。先定一条主线，再决定谁加倍、谁对位、谁只提供节奏。

## 音区与掩蔽

同度最危险，三度与六度通常更清楚。低音区堆得越密，整体越浑；中音区最容易互相遮住。把一方移开八度，或让它改奏长音，往往比换音色更有效。

## 常见误解

以为组合就是找一组好听的音色。其实先有的是关系：前景与背景、厚与薄、动与静。音色只是把这些关系显出来。

## The problem

Two instruments sounding together do not simply add their volumes, and neither keeps its colour intact. Combination is about relationships: who is in front, who is behind, and who fills the gap between them.

## Instrument combination

Three working rules: one strong against one weak, one high against one low, one moving against one still. Instruments of the same family blend easily but offer little contrast. Instruments of different families contrast strongly but must yield in register and dynamics. Fix one main line first, then decide who doubles it, who counters it, and who merely supplies rhythm.

## Register and masking

Unison is the most dangerous spacing; thirds and sixths usually read more clearly. The lower the register, the more density turns to mud, and the middle register masks most readily. Move one part by an octave, or turn it into a sustained note, and the result is often clearer than changing timbre.

## What to listen for

In the First Brandenburg Concerto, hear how three oboes, bassoon, two horns and strings each occupy a layer. Notice who speaks and who merely holds the harmony. The bassoon phrase below marks the low register.

## Common misconceptions

The mistake is to treat combination as finding a set of pleasant colours. Relationships come first: front and back, thick and thin, moving and still. Colour only makes those relationships audible.
