::: zh
引子、过渡与尾奏各自只做一件事。引子建立期待，过渡改变一个参数，尾奏完成或拒绝解决。三处都塞满素材，段落就会失去方向。

## 问题
引子一次亮完全部素材，过渡只靠鼓加花，尾奏突然停止。听众知道换了段，却不知道为什么换。

## 织体或频段
引子常留掉低频或留掉高频，制造未完成。过渡用上行低音、悬置和弦或密度变化指向下一段。尾奏保留一个持续音，让和声慢慢消失，或用强收终止。

## 做法
引子只介绍一个元素，节奏、音色或动机。过渡只改一个参数，音区、和声或密度。尾奏只做一次解决。过渡生硬时先查低音是否提前到达新调。尾奏太短时延长时间，不要只加音量。

## 听什么
听巴赫 BWV 831 法国序曲，附点慢引子后转入快速段。听《图画展览会》的漫步主题如何在乐章间转场。再听《土耳其进行曲》的尾声怎样收住主题。

## 常见误解
引子越长越有气势是错的，短引子更容易抓住注意力。尾奏也不等于渐弱，强收同样成立。

::: en
An intro, a transition and an outro each do one job. The intro builds expectation, the transition changes one parameter, and the outro resolves or refuses to resolve. Fill all three with material and the sections lose their direction.

## The problem
An intro that shows every idea at once, a transition that relies on a drum fill, and an outro that simply stops all leave listeners aware of a change without understanding it.

## Texture or frequency band
Intros often withhold low or high frequencies to create incompleteness. Transitions point forward with a rising bass, a suspended chord or a change of density. Outros hold one sustained tone and let the harmony fade, or close with a firm cadence.

## Approach
Let the intro introduce one element, whether rhythm, timbre or motif. Let the transition change one parameter, whether register, harmony or density. Let the outro resolve once. If a transition feels stiff, check whether the bass arrives in the new key too early. If an outro feels short, extend its duration rather than its volume.

## What to listen for
Hear the dotted slow opening of Bach's French Overture BWV 831 turn into a fast section. Hear the Promenade from Pictures at an Exhibition link the movements. Then hear the coda of the Alla turca close the theme.

## Common misunderstandings
A longer intro is not automatically more impressive, and a short one often grabs attention faster. An outro is not the same as a fade, since a firm ending works just as well.
