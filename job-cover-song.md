## 你要交的是什么

一版翻唱或改编：先决定是照原编曲唱，还是换一种编曲重做。交立体声混音、伴奏版（无人声）、主唱与和声 stems，以及一份写明原曲、词曲作者与改编方式的说明。若原曲有说唱段，先决定保留还是改写，别到录音当天才定。若用于视频或商业场景，另附你已核对的权利文件。文件用 48 kHz、24 bit，伴奏与混音等长、同起点。

## 三步走

先定调与速度：用原曲旋律试唱，找到能唱完副歌而不紧的调，再决定是否改拍速；把调与速度写进工程名，录唱时不再改。第二步做伴奏：照原曲的和声走向搭结构，或明确换一种律动；录主唱，叠和声，段落之间留出换气。第三步混音与核对：人声在前，伴奏让位，导出伴奏版与 stems，再逐条确认词曲、录音与同步权分别由谁授权、覆盖哪些用途。

## 用到的工具与页面

任何 DAW 都行，先读这里的编曲与作曲的分界，以及词典里的机械复制权。用测速工具确认原曲 BPM，用移调插件试调，但最终以真声唱得舒服为准。和声从三度、六度开始写，别一上来就四部。参考曲只用来定风格，不要直接把原唱贴进工程。需要发布时，按平台要求提交元数据与授权信息，并把改编说明留给合作方。

## 验收标准

人声全程在调，副歌不喊；伴奏与主唱同长、同起点；伴奏版里没有残余人声；换气声可以留在混音里，不要全部修掉；改编部分能说清改了哪几样：调、速度、律动、配器；权利人、授权范围与期限写在交付说明里；文件命名含曲名、版本与调性；元数据里的作者与作品名拼写正确。

## 常见返工原因

调定得太高，副歌靠喊；伴奏照搬原版又没拿到授权；只交混音，演出方要伴奏时得重做；人声修得太平，听起来像合成器；权利没理清，发布后被下架；元数据里漏了原作者，平台分账对不上；伴奏比人声早做一版，调没定就开录，最后全部重来；改编幅度太大，听众认不出原曲，却又仍按翻唱宣传。

## What you have to hand over

A cover or a rework: decide first whether you are singing over the original arrangement or rebuilding it. If the original has a rap verse, decide whether to keep or rewrite it before the recording day, not on it. Deliver a stereo mix, an instrumental without voice, lead and harmony stems, and a note naming the original song, its writers and what you changed. If it is used in video or commercially, add the rights documents you have checked. Use 48 kHz and 24 bit, with the instrumental and the mix at equal length and the same start point.

## Three steps

Set key and tempo first. Sing the original melody and find the key where the chorus is not a strain, then decide whether the tempo changes. Second, build the backing: follow the original harmonic path, or state clearly that the groove is new. Record the lead, stack harmonies, and leave room to breathe between sections. Third, mix and verify: voice forward, backing yielding, export the instrumental and stems, then confirm one by one who licenses the composition, the recording and the sync use, and which uses each licence covers.

## Tools and pages

Any DAW will do. Read the site page Arranging Versus Composition first, then the glossary entry Mechanical License. Use a tempo tool to confirm the original BPM and a transpose plug-in to try keys, but let the unaided voice decide. A reference track is for style only; do not paste the original recording into the session. At release, submit the metadata and licence information the platform asks for, and pass the change note to collaborators.

## Acceptance checks

The voice stays in tune and the chorus is not shouted. Instrumental and lead share length and start. The instrumental contains no vocal residue. You can list exactly what the arrangement changed: key, tempo, groove, instrumentation. Rights holder, scope and term appear in the delivery note. File names carry song, version and key. Writer and title spellings in the metadata are correct.

## Why work gets sent back

The key is too high and the chorus is shouted. The backing copies the original without a licence. Only a mix is delivered, so the performance needs a new session. The vocal is tuned so flat that it sounds like a synth. Rights are unclear and the release is taken down. The original writer is missing from the metadata and royalties cannot be matched. The rework drifts so far that listeners cannot recognise the song, yet it is still promoted as a cover.
