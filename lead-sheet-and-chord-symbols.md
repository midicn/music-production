## 问题

爵士乐队、婚礼乐队、录音棚的临时编制，常常没有时间排总谱。领谱用一行旋律加和弦记号，把编配交给演奏者。写得清楚，乐队一遍就能合；写得含糊，每个人都在猜。

## 原理

和弦记号用根音加性质表示和声，如 Cmaj7、Dm7、G7alt。延伸音与变化音写在括号里，斜杠表示转位或指定低音。领谱通常只写旋律、和弦与段落标记，节奏与织体由演奏者按风格决定。同一个记号在不同风格里含义不同，摇摆八分与直八分要另行注明。记号写得越细，自由度越小。

## 参数与做法

先定调与曲式，标清反复与结尾。和弦记号要与实际和声一致，别用简化记号掩盖经过和弦。需要特定低音时用斜杠和弦。速度与风格写在页首。给歌手的谱要标歌词位置与换气。现场用的谱尽量一页放下，减少翻页。录音前确认乐队对记号的理解一致，必要时录一段参考。

## 听什么

在叙事曲主题的领谱版里听旋律骨架是否还认得出，在吉普赛曲调里听装饰音由演奏者现场补出的差别，在赞美诗的和弦记号版里听内声部如何被重新分配。领谱的好坏，取决于它给演奏者留了多少可判断的空间。

## The problem

Jazz bands, wedding groups and ad-hoc studio line-ups rarely have time to rehearse a full score. A lead sheet gives one melody line and chord symbols and hands the arrangement to the players. Written clearly, the band plays it down once; written vaguely, everyone guesses.

## How it works

Chord symbols combine a root with a quality, such as Cmaj7, Dm7 or G7alt. Extensions and alterations go in brackets, and a slash names an inversion or a required bass. A lead sheet usually carries melody, chords and section marks, while rhythm and texture follow from the style. The same symbol means different things in different idioms, so swing eighths and straight eighths must be stated. The more detail the sheet adds, the less freedom it leaves.

## Settings and practice

Fix the key and form first and mark repeats and endings. Chord symbols should match the actual harmony rather than hiding passing chords behind simplifications. Use slash chords when a specific bass is needed. Put tempo and feel at the top. For singers, mark lyric placement and breaths. Keep live charts to one page where possible. Confirm that the band reads the symbols the same way before recording, and cut a reference take if needed.

## What to listen for

On the lead-sheet version of the ballade theme, whether the melodic skeleton is still recognisable; on the gypsy tune, how ornaments differ when players supply them live; on the hymn chart, how inner voices get redistributed. A lead sheet is only as good as the room it leaves for judgement.
