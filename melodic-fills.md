::: zh
旋律之间的空档要不要填，是编曲里最容易被过度处理的地方。加花的目的是把两句连起来，而不是证明演奏者会多少音。

## 问题
每个空档都填满，主题就没有呼吸，听者也会失去对下一句的期待。先判断这个空档需不需要被解释，再决定加不加。

## 织体或频段
加花通常放在旋律长音、休止或句尾。它应该比主题简单，模进、音阶经过、短小动机的重复都可以。若主题在中高音区，加花可以下移到中音区，避免两条线抢同一位置。

## 做法与判断
先标出每句的呼吸点，只在呼吸点前后加。每次加花不超过两拍，音型不超过四个音。同一段落不要每次都用同一种加花，第二次可以只改结尾，第三次留空，让下一次出现更有分量。

## 听什么
听变奏曲主题，第一次陈述通常最朴素，后面才逐渐加装饰。再听民谣或爵士标准曲，句尾的短句往往重复上一句的节奏型，而不是新旋律。

## 常见误解
加花越多越专业是错的。加花占满每个空档，等于取消了呼吸，也取消了主题。

::: en
Whether to fill the gaps between phrases is where arranging is most often overdone. A fill should join two phrases or point attention to the next one, not prove how many notes the player knows.

## The problem
Fill every gap and the theme stops breathing; the listener loses any reason to expect the next phrase. Decide whether the gap needs explaining first.

## Texture or frequency band
Fills belong on long melody notes, rests and phrase endings, occupying the band the melody has vacated. They should be simpler than the theme. If the theme sits high, move the fill down to the middle to avoid two lines competing.

## Approach and judgement
Mark the breathing point of each phrase and add only around it. Keep each fill to two beats and four notes. Do not use the same figure every time, change only the ending on the second pass and leave the third empty.

## What to listen for
In a theme and variations, the first statement is usually plain and decoration arrives later. In folk or jazz standards, the short phrase at a cadence often repeats the previous rhythm rather than introducing new melody.

## Common misunderstandings
More ornament does not mean more professionalism. A fill in every gap cancels the breath and cancels the theme with it.
