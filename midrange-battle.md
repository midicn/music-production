## 问题

高频决定空气感，低频决定体量，但让人听清歌词、分辨乐器、判断攻击的，是八百到五千赫兹。几乎所有轨都在这块区域有能量，于是越加越满，初听刺激，十分钟后只想关掉。

## 目标与判断

中频要有明确的主人。主唱或主奏站在最前，军鼓与吉他轮换出现，铺底让出位置。判断时把音量降到很小，若歌词仍可懂，中频就站住了；若只剩一片嗡嗡声，说明低中频堆积。刺不等于清楚，厚也不等于有力量。

## 做法

先定主角的两到四千赫兹，用窄带小幅衰减竞争声部，而不是把主角推高。四百到八百赫兹的盒子感用宽而浅的衰减处理，避免把乐器掏空。需要密度时加一点饱和，让谐波填补空隙，再回到整段检查。

## 听什么

听辅音是否清楚却不扎耳，军鼓起音是否从吉他后面露出来，长时间聆听是否疲劳。下面四个中音连续出现，注意哪一个最先变得刺。

## The problem

Highs supply air and lows supply weight, but intelligibility, instrument separation and attack all depend on eight hundred to five thousand hertz. Almost every track carries energy there. The mix grows fuller, sounds exciting at first, and becomes exhausting after ten minutes.

## Goal and judgement

The midrange needs a clear owner. The lead vocal or lead instrument stands in front, snare and guitar take turns, and pads step back. Judge at a very low volume. If the words remain intelligible, the midrange holds. If only a blur remains, low-mid energy has piled up. Brightness is not clarity, and thickness is not strength.

## Method

Establish the lead between two and four thousand hertz, then cut the competing parts by a small amount with a narrow band instead of pushing the lead higher. Treat boxiness around four hundred to eight hundred hertz with a wide, shallow cut so instruments are not hollowed out. When density is needed, add a little saturation so harmonics fill the gaps, then check the full section again.

## What to listen for

Check that consonants are clear without stinging, that the snare attack emerges from behind the guitar, and that long listening does not tire you. The four mid notes below repeat; notice which one turns harsh first.

## Common misconceptions

That the midrange is just nasality and should be cut away; that brighter highs mean clearer sound; that a fuller middle means more power.
