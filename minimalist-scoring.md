极简配乐不靠主题堆叠，它把两三个音或一个节奏型反复摆放，让变化发生在音色、速度和层次上。

## 问题

画面很慢时，传统交响写法会显得过满。观众需要的是时间感，而不是另一条情节。极简手法提供了一种不抢戏又能持续存在的音乐。

## 音画关系

重复的段落与长镜头天然合拍。音乐不必跟着每个动作，它只要维持一个状态，等画面自己发生变化。当剧情转折时，加一件乐器或换一个和弦，就足以被听成事件。

## 做法与判断

先选一个短细胞，长度不超过两小节。用固定音型打底，每次只改一个参数：加一层、移一个音、换一次音色。判断标准是十分钟后是否仍然不烦，若中途需要靠旋律救场，说明细胞本身不够耐听。

## 听什么

听《i giorni》的左手音型怎样几乎不变，右手却慢慢换位置；听《裸体歌舞》第一首怎样用休止制造空间；听氛围循环里有没有真正的旋律。演示用钢琴重复一个四音音型，再在高声部加一个音，听感会从静止变成推进。

## 常见误解

极简不等于简单，重复对演奏的稳定性要求更高。也不需要从头到尾一个材料，段落之间仍要有对比。另一点，极简配乐常被误认为没有情绪，其实情绪藏在音色与混响里。

Minimalist scoring does not stack themes. It places two or three notes, or one rhythm, in repetition and lets change happen through timbre, speed and layers.

## The problem

When the picture is slow, a traditional orchestral approach feels overfull. The audience needs a sense of time, not a second plot. Minimalism stays present without competing for attention.

## Music and picture

Repetition sits naturally against long takes. The music need not follow every gesture; it holds a state while the image changes on its own. At a turn, adding one instrument or changing one chord is heard as an event.

## Practice and judgement

Choose a short cell, no longer than two bars. Lay down a fixed figure and change one parameter at a time: add a layer, move a note, swap a timbre. The test is whether it still feels fresh after ten minutes. If it needs a melody to rescue it midway, the cell itself is not durable enough.

## What to listen for

In i giorni, the left-hand figure barely changes while the right hand gradually shifts position. Hear how the first Gymnopédie uses rests to make space, and whether the ambient loop contains a real melody at all. The demo repeats a four-note figure, then moves only the top note; the stillness turns into a push.

## Common misunderstandings

Minimal is not simple; repetition demands steadiness from a player. Nor does the whole cue need one material, since sections still need contrast. It is often called emotionless, yet the feeling lives in timbre and reverb.
