## 难在哪

画面剪好了，音乐却对不上：该在切换点落下的和弦早了两拍，该延长的地方音乐已经收尾。贴片剪辑就是让两者对齐。它既不是简单地把音乐拖来拖去，也不是让画面迁就音乐，而是在节奏、和声与画面之间找可接受的落点。

## 原理

音乐有句法：乐句、和声进行与终止式形成听觉上的标点。剪辑点落在标点上，听起来自然；落在句中，会像话说到一半被打断。速度变化、延长与重复是常用手段，但每次改动都会影响后续段落的时间。调性变化处不宜硬切，音量骤变也需要过渡。最稳的做法是让音乐先写对，剪辑只做微调。

## 参数与做法

先标出画面关键点，再标出音乐的句法与和声落点，找两者最接近的位置。需要缩短时优先删重复段，需要延长时优先加过渡或延长尾音。变速幅度小一点，超过几个百分点就会听出音色变化。用淡入淡出处理不可避免的硬切，别让切口落在重音上。改完通看一遍，确认整体节奏没被破坏。

## 耳朵要抓的点

在第二乐章的句尾听音乐标点是否与画面同落，在四重奏的呼吸处听切口是否落在句中，在持续音段落里听延长是否自然。好的贴片让人感觉音乐本来就是这样写的。

## Where it gets hard

Picture is cut but the music will not fit: the chord meant for a cut arrives two beats early, the passage that should extend has already ended. Music editing lines the two up. It is neither dragging music around nor making picture serve the music, but finding acceptable landing points among rhythm, harmony and image.

## Principle

Music has syntax: phrases, harmonic motion and cadences create audible punctuation. A cut on punctuation feels natural; a cut mid-phrase sounds like an interruption. Tempo changes, extensions and repeats are common tools, but each one shifts everything after it in time. Hard cuts across a key change do not work, and sudden level changes need transitions. The surest approach is to write the music correctly and let editing make only small adjustments.

## Settings and practice

Mark picture events first, then mark musical phrase and harmonic landing points, and match the closest pair. To shorten, drop a repeated section first; to lengthen, add a transition or extend a tail. Keep tempo changes small, since a few percent already alters timbre. Fade unavoidable hard cuts and never place one on an accent. Watch the whole sequence afterwards to confirm the overall pace survives.

## What the ear catches

In the slow movement, whether musical punctuation lands with the picture; in the quartet's breaths, whether cuts fall inside phrases; in the sustained passage, whether extension feels natural. Good editing makes the music seem written that way.
