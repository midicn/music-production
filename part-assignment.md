::: zh
同一组和弦，为什么有的编曲每条声部都在说话，有的却像一堵墙。差别常在声部分配，谁拿旋律、谁填内声部、谁负责低音、谁在休止。

## 问题
所有声部都唱满，和声反而听不清。安排声部就是给每个音高找到负责的人，并允许有人暂时不说话。

## 织体或频段
四声部写作把音域分成高、中、中低、低四带。旋律不必永远在最上面，它可以下沉到中音区，让上方声部应答。内声部最难写，因为它既要补和弦音，又不能与旋律抢同一频段。

## 做法与判断
先定主旋律的位置，再让其余声部按最近距离移动，避免大跳与声部交叉。两条声部同向时容易糊，反向或斜向更清楚。若某声部长时间只重复和弦音，让它休止一小节，比继续填满更有用。

## 听什么
听合唱或弦乐四重奏的慢乐章，注意旋律换到哪件乐器时，其他声部会退到长音或停顿。再听主调流行歌，内声部往往被压成一条持续音。

## 常见误解
声部多不代表安排得好。四支旋律同时抢高音区，比三声部清楚的作品更浑浊。

::: en
The same chords can sound as if every part speaks, or like a wall. The difference is part assignment, meaning who takes the melody, who fills the inner voices, who holds the bass, and who rests.

## The problem
When every part sings continuously, the harmony becomes less clear. Assignment means giving each pitch a responsible player and allowing someone to fall silent.

## Texture or frequency band
Four-part writing divides the range into four bands. The melody need not stay on top; it can drop into the middle while an upper voice answers. Inner parts are hardest, since they must complete the chord without crowding the melody.

## Approach and judgement
Fix the melody's position first, then move the other parts by the nearest interval, avoiding leaps and voice crossing. Similar motion blurs; contrary motion stays clear. If a part repeats chord tones for bars, one bar of rest helps more than another note.

## What to listen for
In a choral piece or a slow quartet movement, notice how other parts fall back to long notes when the melody changes instrument. In a homophonic pop song the inner voices are often reduced to one sustained line.

## Common misunderstandings
More parts do not mean better part-writing. Four lines all fighting in the top register sound muddier than a clear three-part texture.
