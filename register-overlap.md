## 问题

两件乐器的音域重叠是常态。重叠本身不是问题，冲突来自同音区、同音型、同时间。配器要做的不是消灭重叠，而是管理交接。

## 乐器组合

让交叠区变成交接区：一件乐器在它最舒服的音区退出，另一件接上。弦乐与木管、圆号与巴松、长号与大提琴都是常见交接对。交接时音色会变，正好用来标记句子的转折。

## 音区与掩蔽

低音区重叠最危险，中音区最容易掩蔽，高音区较容易分开。用八度、节奏或力度把两者分开。若两件乐器必须同度，让一方奏长音，另一方奏动型。

## 听什么

《第四十交响曲》的木管与弦乐交叠，听谁在什么时候接过线条；《第一勃兰登堡》的圆号与双簧管，听两种音色在同一音区怎样分开；维尔纳《第一交响曲》改编版的圆号与巴松，听低中音区交接。

## 常见误解

以为音区重叠必须避免。处理得当，交叠能让音色过渡更平滑；真正要避免的是没有安排的撞车。

## The problem

Overlapping ranges are normal. Overlap itself is not the problem; collision comes from the same register, the same figure and the same moment. Scoring should manage the handover, not try to abolish overlap.

## Instrument combination

Turn the overlap into a handover: one instrument leaves at the top of its comfortable register and another takes over. Strings with woodwind, horn with bassoon, trombone with cello are common pairs. The colour changes at the handover, which is useful for marking a turn in the phrase.

## Register and masking

Overlap is most dangerous in the bass, most masking in the middle, and easier to separate at the top. Use octaves, rhythm or dynamics to keep the parts apart. If two instruments must share a unison, let one sustain while the other moves.

## What to listen for

In the Fortieth Symphony, hear woodwind and strings overlap and notice who takes the line. In the First Brandenburg Concerto, hear horn and oboe separate within one register. In the orchestral arrangement of Vierne's First Symphony, hear horn and bassoon hand over in the low-middle range.

## Common misconceptions

Overlap is not something to avoid at all costs. Handled well, it makes a colour change smoother; what must be avoided is an unplanned collision.
