同样的音，放在不同八度，重量和清晰度完全不同。音区安排决定主题出现在哪里、低音下探多深。

## 问题
主题写得过高会紧，写得过低会糊。音区没有规划时，每件乐器都在自己最舒服的地方演奏，合起来却互相遮挡。

## 织体或频段
人耳对中高音区的旋律最敏感，低音区提供根基，但过多会浑。把主题放在乐器最有把握的音区，比勉强唱高音可靠。低音与旋律之间留出一个八度以上的空档，和声才有地方站。

## 做法与判断
先定旋律音区，再让低音至少低一个八度。内声部填在两者之间，但不要贴着旋律。整首曲子的音区可以随段落移动，主歌窄，副歌上下同时扩张，这是最省力的抬升。只在极低或极高音区出现的乐器，每次出现都会很显眼。

## 听什么
听管风琴作品，脚踏板与手键盘的音区差距一出来，空间感立刻不同。再听弦乐四重奏，大提琴换到高把位时，低音支撑会暂时消失。

## 常见误解
音区高不等于响，低也不等于厚。写得太低，小音箱上会直接消失。

The same note carries different weight and clarity in another octave. Register planning decides where the theme appears, how deep the bass reaches, and whether the piece has any ceiling.

## The problem
A theme written too high sounds tight; too low and it blurs. Without a plan, each instrument plays where it is comfortable and the result masks itself.

## Texture or frequency band
The ear is most sensitive to melody in the upper-middle range, while the bass provides foundation and turns muddy in excess. Put the theme where the instrument is secure, and leave an octave between bass and melody so harmony has room.

## Approach and judgement
Fix the melody register first, then place the bass at least an octave below. Fill inner parts between them, but not against the melody. The whole range can move by section, narrow in the verse and expanding up and down in the chorus, which is the cheapest lift.

## What to listen for
In organ music, the gap between pedals and manuals changes the sense of space at once. In a string quartet, listen for the moment the cello moves high and the bass support briefly vanishes.

## Common misunderstandings
High does not mean loud, and low does not mean thick. Write too low and the note disappears on small speakers.
