::: zh
一段编曲让人想点头，靠的往往是节奏密度在段落之间的变化，而不是某件乐器有多花。密度是单位时间里发声事件的多少，以及这些事件落在哪些拍点上。

## 问题
所有声部一起加密，听感只会变吵，不会变快。密度失控时，先找哪一层本可以不动。

## 织体或频段
低频适合稀，高频可以密。底鼓与贝斯若都塞满十六分，低频会糊成一片；闭合镲可以连续跑动而不占低频空间。密度还要看留白，同样八小节，有两拍空出来的版本通常更有推动力。

## 做法与判断
副歌前一小节减少事件，让进入更明显。副歌里保持底鼓稳定，把变化交给高频与打击乐。若所有声部同时加密，段落只会变重。用八分与十六分的对比安排段落，比单纯提高速度自然。

## 听什么
先只听鼓组，数每小节的事件数，再听全曲，注意贝斯是否跟着鼓一起加密。两者同步加密时，低频最容易失控。

## 常见误解
密度高不等于能量高。真正推得动的段落，常常是低频稀、高频密。

::: en
A groove makes people nod because rhythmic density changes between sections, not because one instrument is busy. Density is the number of sounding events per unit of time, and the beats they land on.

## The problem
When every part thickens at once, the result gets louder rather than faster. If density is out of control, find the layer that could have stayed still.

## Texture or frequency band
Low frequencies tolerate sparseness; highs can be dense. If kick and bass both fill sixteenths, the low end turns to mud, while a closed hi-hat can run without occupying that space. In the same eight bars, a version with two beats left open usually pushes harder.

## Approach and judgement
Thin the events in the bar before a chorus so the entrance is obvious. Keep the kick steady through the chorus and give change to the highs and percussion. If everything thickens together, the section only gets heavier.

## What to listen for
Listen to the drums alone and count events per bar, then to the full mix and check whether the bass thickens with them. When both do, the low end is the first thing to lose control.

## Common misunderstandings
High density is not high energy. The sections that really push often keep the low end sparse and the top busy.
