## 问题

一段两秒的鼓点，为什么不能直接拿来用？

## 原理

录音制品上通常叠着两层权利。一层是作品权，属于作曲与作词；另一层是录音制作者权，属于把声音录下来的人。采样同时用到这两层，所以要分别获得许可。公有领域的作品，其现代录音仍可能受保护；反过来，一份标注开放的编曲文件，也可能对应一首仍在版权期的作品。许可条款决定能做什么，署名、相同方式共享与非商业限制都会影响发行。合理使用在多数地区是抗辩而非授权，不能当作默认许可。

## 做法与参数

先确认素材来源与许可文本，保存页面与下载日期。分清作品与录音，分别找权利人。需要署名时，把署名写进元数据与发行说明。修改后是否要共享，取决于原许可。商用前做一次清权清单，列出素材、来源、许可、义务与期限。拿不准时换素材，比事后补救便宜。

## 听什么

听成品里的采样，判断它是否可辨认。可辨认的旋律与音色更容易被认出，也更容易引发争议。听经过变调与切片后的片段，确认它是否仍能追溯到原曲。听编曲层，判断你用的是旋律、节奏型还是音色本身。

## 常见误解

以为标注免费就等于可以商用；以为改过音高就无需清权；以为短采样一定安全。

## The problem

Why can a two-second drum loop not simply be dropped into a track?

## How it works

A recording usually carries two layers of rights. One is the composition, owned by the writer; the other is the recording, owned by the person or company that captured the sound. Sampling uses both, so both need clearance. A public-domain work can still have a protected recording; conversely, an open file may encode a work still in copyright. The licence sets what is allowed: credit, share-alike and non-commercial terms all shape release. Fair use is a defence in some jurisdictions, not a default permission.

## Practice and settings

Identify the source and the licence text, and keep a copy with the download date. Separate composition from recording and approach each rights holder. Where credit is required, put it in metadata and release notes. Whether changes must be shared depends on the original licence. Before release, build a clearance list with material, source, licence, obligations and expiry. When in doubt, replace the sample; it is cheaper than repairing later.

## What to listen for

Hear the sample in the finished track and judge if it is recognisable. Recognisable melodies and timbres are identified easily and challenged often. Hear the processed fragment and check whether it still traces back. Hear the arrangement layer and decide whether you borrowed a melody, a groove or a timbre.

## Common misconceptions

That a free download is free to use commercially; that pitch shifting removes the need to clear; that short samples are always safe.
