## 问题

录音当天最贵的不是设备，是所有人的时间。准备清单的作用，是把可以提前决定的事全部提前，让到场的人只做演奏。

## 信号链

清单从作品开始：定调、定速度、定编制，再到话筒、线材、耳机返送与文件命名。每一项都在开录之前确认，避免现场临时改方案，把时间耗在决定上。

## 摆位与参数

建立输入表，逐项写清通道号、乐器/人名、话筒、是否需幻象供电、轨名与返送去向。给参考轨标版本和速度变化；需要自由速度的段落，事先约定口令或引入方式。建立工程后做保存、关闭和重新打开测试，并确认所有素材确实写入项目目录。

## 听什么

开录前先录三十秒试音，回听整条链：有没有哼声、串音、相位问题，返送里能不能听清自己。确认之后再请演奏者正式进入。

## 常见误解

- **清单是给新手用的** → 经验越多，越清楚漏项的代价。
- **试音可以省** → 问题留到正式录音，代价是整条重录。
- **准备越久越好** → 该准备的是决定，不是设备堆叠。

## The problem

On a session day the most expensive thing is not gear but everyone's time. A prep list exists to decide in advance everything that can be decided, so the people in the room only have to play.

## Signal chain

The list starts with the music: key, tempo, instrumentation. Then microphones, cables, headphone mixes and file naming. Every item is confirmed before the first take, so nothing has to be redesigned on the floor while the clock runs.

## Placement and settings

Create an input sheet with channel, instrument or performer, microphone, phantom-power requirement, track name and monitor destination. Version the reference track and mark tempo changes; agree a count-in or cue for free-time sections. Save, close and reopen the session, confirming that its media are actually inside the project directory.

## What to listen for

Record thirty seconds before the session and check the whole chain: hum, bleed, phase, and whether each player can hear themselves in the cue. Only then invite the performers in.

## Common misconceptions

- **Checklists are for beginners** → experience shows what a missing item costs.
- **A sound check can be skipped** → the problem then appears during the take.
- **Longer preparation is always better** → you prepare decisions, not piles of gear.
