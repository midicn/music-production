::: zh
快捷键不是炫技，是把「找菜单」这段时间省下来，留给耳朵。

## 问题

剪一段鼓，先选工具、再找菜单、再确认对话框，声音早就走神了。重复动作一多，注意力就从听感转移到操作上，错误也跟着来。

## 做法

先列出手上最常重复的五件事，例如切开、复制、缩放到选区、开关节拍器、独奏。每周只加五个键位，在真实工程里用，不用专门练。把常用功能收进一套自己的键位表，避开系统与输入法的冲突。宏要在动作稳定后再录，否则会把坏习惯批量复制。

## 参数与判断

一只手管键盘、一只手管鼠标，减少两只手来回换。组合键不超过三个键，相邻功能放在相邻手指下。与走带、缩放相关的键位优先，因为它们用得最多。换电脑时先导出键位表，再在新机器导入。

## 听什么

连续编辑十分钟，检查自己是否还能边操作边听。若每次切工具都要停一下，说明键位还没进肌肉记忆。用同一段循环计时，比较熟练前后的编辑耗时。

```audiolab
{"type":"rhythm","sig":"4/4","pattern":"e e e e e e e e","bpm":100,"label":"重复的八分网格","label_en":"A repeating eighth grid","hint":"想象每一格都要切一刀，连续做二十次","hint_en":"Picture cutting every slot twenty times in a row"}
```

## 常见误解

键位越多越快，其实记不住的键位等于没有。默认键位不是标准，换 DAW 时该带走的是习惯而不是表。快捷键也救不了混乱的工程结构。

::: en
Shortcuts are not a display of skill. They buy back the time spent hunting menus and give it to listening.

## The problem

You cut a loop, reach for a tool, open a menu, confirm a dialog, and the sound has moved on. When the same action repeats, attention shifts from hearing to operating, and mistakes follow.

## The practice

List the five actions you repeat most, such as split, duplicate, zoom to selection, toggle the click and solo. Add five keys a week and use them in a real session, not in drills. Collect frequent commands into one keymap and avoid clashes with the system and input methods. Record a macro only after the action is stable, or it copies a bad habit at scale.

## Settings and judgement

Keep one hand on keys and one on the mouse, so neither travels. Limit combinations to three keys and place related functions under neighbouring fingers. Prioritise transport and zoom keys, because they are used most. Export the keymap before changing computers.

## What to listen for

Edit for ten minutes and check whether you can still hear while operating. If every tool change forces a pause, the keys are not yet muscle memory. Time the same loop before and after.

## Common misunderstandings

More shortcuts do not mean more speed; keys you cannot recall are keys you do not have. The default map is not a standard, and what you carry to a new DAW is the habit, not the table. Shortcuts also cannot rescue a badly organised project.
