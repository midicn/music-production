::: zh
留白是编曲材料。音符之间的空，让混响尾巴、房间声和下一句的重音有位置。全部填满时，听众抓不住重点。所有层同时呼吸时，音乐又会停住。

## 问题
每个小节都塞满，副歌前没有落点，下一段只能靠更大声来区分。留白不是少写，而是把重音留给该出现的地方。

## 织体或频段
留白有三种。频段空，去掉中低频让高频尾音显露。层数空，只留持续音。时间空，在乐句尾休止。低频停顿时，高频的余响会撑住空间，完全静音反而像断线。

## 做法
先标出旋律的换气点，再让伴奏在这些点减少事件。副歌前留半小节或一整拍，比加过门有效。用持续音或混响托住空白，避免硬切。若一段听起来累，先删每小节后两拍的和声层。

## 听什么
听萨蒂《裸体歌舞》第一首和弦之间的长空隙。听巴赫第三无伴奏大提琴组曲萨拉班德的句尾。再听氛围循环，数一数一分钟里只有多少音符。

## 常见误解
留白不等于安静，它需要一点持续声维持空间。留白也不是偷懒，删掉什么、留下什么都要有判断。

::: en
Space is an arranging material. The gaps between notes give reverb tails, room sound and the next accent somewhere to sit. When every bar is full, listeners lose the emphasis. When every layer breathes at once, the music stops moving.

## The problem
Fill every bar and the pre-chorus has no landing point, so the next section can only be louder. Space is not less writing. It reserves the accent for the moment that needs it.

## Texture or frequency band
Space can be vertical or horizontal. Empty a band by removing low-mid content so high tails show. Empty the layers by keeping one sustained tone. Empty time with rests at phrase ends. When the low end stops, high reverberation holds the room, while complete silence sounds like a broken connection.

## Approach
Mark the melody's breathing points and reduce events in the accompaniment there. Half a bar or one beat of space before a chorus works better than a fill. Sustain a tone or let reverb cover the gap instead of cutting hard. If a passage feels tiring, remove the harmony layer on the last two beats of each bar.

## What to listen for
Hear the long gaps between chords in Satie's first Gymnopedie. Hear the phrase endings in the Sarabande of Bach's third cello suite. In an ambient loop, count how few notes carry a whole minute.

## Common misunderstandings
Space is not silence, since it needs a sustained sound or tail to keep the room open. It is not laziness either, because deciding what to remove still requires judgement.
