## 问题

弦乐分谱不是把和弦写成五条长音。要决定分成几个声部、谁奏旋律、谁换弓、谁在低音。谱面写的是动作，听到的才是结果。

## 乐器组合

第一、第二小提琴、中提琴、大提琴与低音提琴构成常规五部。分奏让和声更密，但每个声部更薄；齐奏更有重量，却少了内部流动。中提琴常被低估，它填的正是和声最挤的那一层。

## 音区与掩蔽

中提琴容易与大提琴、第二小提琴重叠；低音区堆得太密会浑。把内声部简化，比继续加音更有效。弓法与力度要写清楚，否则分谱到了排练场只能靠猜。

## 听什么

贝多芬《第八弦乐四重奏》第一乐章，听四个声部怎样交换角色；《第十五弦乐四重奏》的第一乐章，听内声部怎样支撑旋律；《第五交响曲》的弦乐齐奏，听整组的声音怎样合成一块。

## 常见误解

以为弦乐可以无限分奏。分得越多，每个声部越弱，音准也越难；很多时候，减少声部反而让音乐更清楚。

## The problem

Writing for strings is not turning a chord into five sustained lines. It means deciding how many parts there are, who carries the melody, who changes bow, and who holds the bass. The page describes actions; the ear hears the result.

## Instrument combination

First and second violins, viola, cello and double bass make the standard five parts. Divisi thickens the harmony but thins each line; unison has more weight but less inner motion. The viola is often undervalued, yet it fills the layer where the harmony is most crowded.

## Register and masking

The viola easily overlaps cello and second violin, and too much density low down turns muddy. Simplifying an inner part is more effective than adding notes. Bowing and dynamics must be written out, or the section will guess at the first rehearsal.

## What to listen for

In the first movement of Beethoven's Eighth Quartet, hear the four parts exchange roles. In the first movement of the Fifteenth, hear the inner parts support the melody. In the Fifth Symphony, hear a string tutti fuse into one body.

## Common misconceptions

Strings cannot be divided indefinitely. The more parts, the weaker each one and the harder the intonation; often the clearer choice is to use fewer parts.
