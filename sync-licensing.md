::: zh
配乐进入画面前，先要确认谁能授权。一首歌通常叠着三层权利：作品、录音与表演，由不同的人收费。

## 问题

剧组常以为买了唱片就能随便用。唱片含作曲与录音两重权利，电视播出还涉及表演权。游戏、预告片与流媒体的授权范围各不相同，漏掉一层就会在下架或索赔时暴露。

## 流程

清权沿一条链走。同步权把音乐与画面绑定，母带权针对特定录音，表演权由集体管理组织收取。歌剧等舞台作品还涉及整部作品的上演权，与影视同步分开算。公有领域的作品可自由改编，但具体录音仍受保护。

## 做法与判断

先列出使用方式：院线、电视、流媒体、预告片、游戏内。再确认音乐是否原创、是否改编、是否用现成录音。把曲与录音分开问，任何一边没清完都不要进终混。

## 听什么

听《舍赫拉查德》主题在不同录音里如何被演绎，同一份总谱会因演奏与录音不同而对应不同的授权对象；听《西部往事》主题的辨识度，越易认出的材料越要提前清权。演示用钢琴弹一个短主题，它只是曲，不含录音。

## 常见误解

买唱片不等于买到同步权。公有领域也不等于录音免费。另一点，游戏内使用常算交互使用，条款与线性影视不同，不能直接套用。

::: en
Before music reaches the screen, someone must establish who can license it. A song usually carries three layers of rights: composition, recording and performance, each paid to a different party.

## The problem

Teams often assume that buying a record clears the music. A record contains composition and recording rights, and television adds performance rights. Games, trailers and streaming fall under different scopes, and one missing layer surfaces as a takedown or a claim.

## The workflow

Clearance follows a chain. A sync licence ties music to picture, a master licence covers a recording, and societies collect performance royalties. Staged works such as opera involve grand rights, counted apart. A public-domain composition can be rearranged, but a particular recording stays protected.

## Practice and judgement

List the uses: cinema, television, streaming, trailer, in-game. Confirm whether the music is original, whether it will be arranged, and whether an existing recording is used. Ask about composition and recording separately, and do not enter the final mix until both are clear.

## What to listen for

Hear the Scheherazade theme interpreted across different recordings; one score creates a different licensed object with each performance and recording. Hear how recognisable the Once Upon a Time in the West theme is; familiar material needs earlier clearance. ## Common misunderstandings

Buying a record is not buying sync rights. Public domain does not free recordings either. In-game use is often treated as interactive, with terms that differ from film and cannot simply be copied.
