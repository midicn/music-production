## 问题

同一首曲子，给不同的人要用不同的谱。古典演奏者读五线谱，吉他手常看六线谱，乐队排练常拿和弦谱。选错谱型，演奏者要么读不动，要么被过多细节束缚。

## 原理

五线谱用音高位置表达音高，读谱需要训练，但能表达和声、声部与配器的完整信息。六线谱用六条线代表六根弦，数字代表品位，直接告诉手指按哪里，但同一音高可以在不同位置演奏，六线谱把选择固定下来，也丢掉了五线谱的音高直观。和弦谱只写和弦名与节奏，演奏者自行决定织体。三种写法表达的信息量递减，自由度递增。

## 参数与做法

给古典或室内乐写五线谱，给吉他手写六线谱时加节奏记号。乐队排练用和弦谱，标清段落与反复。六线谱要注明调弦与变调夹位置。和弦谱写清转位与延伸音，别只写个大三和弦。交付时附一份总谱或录音，让演奏者知道整体走向。记谱的目标是让演奏者少猜，不是把所有细节写死。

## 听什么

在传统曲调的吉他版里听装饰音落在哪根弦，在里尔舞曲的反复段里听和声骨架是否清楚，在单声部旋律里听记谱给演奏者留了多少自由。谱面越简，演奏者的判断越重要。

## The problem

One piece needs different pages for different players. Classical players read the staff, guitarists often read tablature, and bands rehearse from chord charts. Choose the wrong one and the player either cannot read it or is buried in detail they do not need.

## How it works

The staff places pitch vertically, needs training to read, and can express harmony, parts and orchestration in full. Tablature gives six lines for six strings with numbers for frets, telling the fingers exactly where to go, but since the same pitch exists in several places it fixes one choice and loses the staff's direct pitch picture. A chord chart gives only chord names and rhythm, leaving texture to the player. The three formats carry decreasing information and increasing freedom.

## Settings and practice

Write staff notation for classical and chamber work, and add rhythmic stems when giving guitarists tablature. Use chord charts for band rehearsals with sections and repeats marked. Note tuning and capo position in tablature. Write inversions and extensions in chord charts instead of bare triads. Attach a score or a recording so players understand the overall shape. Notation exists to reduce guessing, not to pin down every detail.

## What to listen for

On the guitar version of a traditional tune, where the ornament lands on the neck; in the reel's repeats, whether the harmonic skeleton is clear; in a single-line melody, how much freedom the notation leaves. The simpler the page, the more the player's judgement matters.
