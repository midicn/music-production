::: zh
临时音轨是剪辑时贴上去的现成音乐。它让导演听到画面该有的节奏，也让作曲家看到对方真正想要什么。

## 问题

临时轨用得太久，全组会把它当成标准答案。原创若与它不同，就会被说不像。更麻烦的是，它的句法与画面剪点绑死，照抄就失去自己的结构。

## 音画关系

先拆临时轨：它给的是速度、配器密度、情绪走向，还是某个同步点。分开记录，再决定哪些保留、哪些换别的材料实现。速度与能量通常保留，旋律与音色必须换掉。

## 做法与判断

先做一份对照表，列出每段的 tempo、织体与关键落点。写原创按功能写，不按旋律写。试听时同时放临时轨与原创，指出保留与替换的部分。若导演仍要相似，把相似限定在能量曲线，而不是主题。

## 听什么

听《野蜂飞舞》的连续音型怎样写死速度感，听《在山妖大厅中》怎样靠加厚与加速完成 build，再听小镇曲的短循环怎样留出重复空间。演示用钢琴弹一条上行固定音型，没有旋律，却已规定画面推进。

## 常见误解

临时轨不是抄袭对象，功能相似不等于材料相似，也不必完全避开它的写法。观众记住的是效果，不是音符。另一点，临时轨的版权同样要清，试映也属于同步。

::: en
A temp track is existing music dropped onto the cut so everyone hears what the scene might feel like. It shows the director the rhythm the picture needs, and tells the composer what is wanted.

## The problem

Leave it too long and the team treats it as the answer. An original cue that differs is called wrong. Worse, its phrasing is locked to the edit, so copying it means losing your structure.

## Music and picture

Take the temp apart. Is it supplying speed, orchestral density, a direction of feeling, or one sync point? Record them separately, then decide what stays and what is achieved otherwise. Tempo and energy stay; melody and timbre must go.

## Practice and judgement

List tempo, texture and key landing points. Write the original by function, not by melody. Play both side by side, naming what you kept and what you replaced. If the director still wants similarity, limit it to the energy curve, not the theme.

## What to listen for

Hear Flight of the Bumblebee's running figures fix a sense of speed, In the Hall of the Mountain King build by thickening and accelerating, and the short town loop leave room to repeat. The piano demo is an ascending fixed figure with no melody that already dictates the push.

## Common misunderstandings

A temp track is not a model to copy; similar function is not similar material. You need not avoid its devices; audiences remember the effect, not the notes. Its rights still need clearing, because a test screening is a synchronization too.
