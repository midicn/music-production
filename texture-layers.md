::: zh
一段音乐听起来满或空，往往不是音轨数量的问题，而是同时发声的东西没有分层。先回答这一刻有几层在动，各自做什么。

## 问题
层数不清时，加音轨只会更糊。先判断哪些声音承担同一功能，才能决定留下谁。

## 织体或频段
常见分层是低音、和声、节奏、旋律，再加一条对位或装饰层。层不等于轨道，两把提琴齐奏同一旋律只算一层，钢琴左右手也可能同时占两层。层与层靠音区、音色和节奏型分开，而不是靠音量硬推。

## 做法与判断
先写下每层的功能，再删掉功能重复的层。旋律层留出容易记住的轮廓，和声层用长音或分解音型并避开旋律的换气点，低音层保住根音与拍点。层数增加时，每层的信息量要减少。

## 听什么
独奏或纯钢琴版本里，旋律与伴奏的界线最清楚。换成四重奏后，跟着同一条旋律，听它怎样从一件乐器交到另一层。若所有层同时做切分，重心会消失。

## 常见误解
层多不等于丰富，齐奏也不等于加厚。让织体变厚的，是各层在音区与节奏上互相让路。

::: en
A passage sounds full or empty less because of track count than because its simultaneous parts were never separated into layers. Arranging starts by asking how many layers move and what each does.

## The problem
When the layer count is unclear, adding tracks only muddies things. Decide which sounds share a function before deciding which to keep.

## Texture or frequency band
The usual layers are bass, harmony, rhythm and melody, plus an optional counterline. A layer is not a track, since two violins in unison are one, and a piano's hands may occupy two. Layers separate through register, timbre and rhythm, not volume.

## Approach and judgement
Name each layer's function, then delete duplicates. Keep the melody's contour easy to remember, put harmony in long notes that avoid its breathing points, and let the bass hold roots and beats. As layers increase, each should carry less information.

## What to listen for
A solo or piano version shows the melody and accompaniment border most clearly. In a quartet or orchestra, follow one melody as it passes from instrument to layer. If every layer syncopates at once, the centre disappears.

## Common misunderstandings
More layers do not mean a richer texture, and unison is not thickness. What thickens a texture is each layer yielding register and rhythm to the others.
