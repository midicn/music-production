## 问题

两件乐器同时响，听觉要么把它们融成一个音色，要么让一方盖住另一方。融合靠泛音重叠与起音一致，掩蔽靠能量与时间重叠。

## 乐器组合

同族乐器最容易融合，木管与木管、弦乐与弦乐都是如此。跨族组合提供对比，但要让音量与发音方式匹配：铜管弱奏可以贴近木管，木管强奏却很难压过铜管。弦乐常作黏合剂，把两组不同的音色缝在一起。

## 音区与掩蔽

掩蔽发生在听觉临界带重叠的地方。低频的掩蔽范围宽，高频较窄，所以低音区的拥挤最难救。把冲突声部移开八度、改变节奏密度，或让一方暂时退出，都比单纯加音量有效。

## 听什么

莫扎特《第四十交响曲》的木管和弦，听双簧管、单簧管与巴松怎样合成一块；《第四勃兰登堡》里竖笛与弦乐交替，听谁穿过谁；《第五交响曲》里木管与弦乐的呼应，听木管怎样在不遮盖弦乐的情况下被听见。

## 常见误解

以为音色越厚越好。厚度常来自重复，清晰却来自分工。两件乐器都奏满，结果往往不是更丰富，而是更模糊。

## The problem

When two instruments sound at once, the ear either fuses them into one colour or lets one cover the other. Fusion depends on overlapping partials and matched attacks; masking depends on overlapping energy in time.

## Instrument combination

Instruments of one family blend most easily, as woodwind with woodwind and strings with strings. Cross-family pairs give contrast, but loudness and attack must be matched: brass at a soft dynamic can sit close to woodwind, while woodwind at full force cannot overpower brass. Strings often act as the glue that stitches two different colours together.

## Register and masking

Masking occurs where critical bands overlap. Low frequencies mask over a wide span, high frequencies over a narrower one, which is why crowding in the bass is hardest to rescue. Move the conflicting part by an octave, change its rhythmic density, or let one part drop out; all work better than raising the volume.

## What to listen for

In Mozart's Fortieth Symphony, hear how oboe, clarinet and bassoon fuse into a woodwind chord. In the Fourth Brandenburg Concerto, hear the recorders and strings alternate and notice which one cuts through. In the Fifth Symphony, hear woodwind answer the strings without covering them.

## Common misconceptions

The assumption that thicker is better is wrong. Thickness often comes from duplication, while clarity comes from division of labour. Two instruments both playing everything usually sound not richer but blurrier.
