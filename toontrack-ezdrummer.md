## 它能完成什么

给 demo 快速配一套能听的鼓，不必自己编每一击。用 groove 浏览器按风格与 BPM 找律动，拖进工程再改。用 Tap2Find 敲一段节奏，让它找相近的律动。用 Bandmate 按参考曲找鼓组。用 Song Track 排主歌、副歌与过门。它也能给现场演出当鼓机。做广告与短视频时，它能在半小时内交出可用鼓轨。用电鼓演奏自己的节奏。把鼓分轨导出给混音。

## 它怎么工作

采样鼓加预制混音：每套鼓自带 room、压缩与 EQ 的成品感，打开就能用。核心库十几 GB，装完即可用，加载通常在几秒内；采样按鼓件与力度分层。混音预设可以整体换，也能只换某个鼓件。MIDI 律动按风格、拍号与复杂度组织，可整体移调到工程，拖拽后仍保留原律动的力度与装饰音。鼓件通过 keyswitch 与 CC4 控制开镲，电鼓可映射。多输出能把底鼓、军鼓、room 分开。

## 边界与坑

第一，声音已经混过：想换军鼓、调 bleed、重做 room，得上 Superior Drummer 3。第二，麦克风与泄漏控制有限，做精细鼓混音会不够。第三，groove 直接用会太标准，要改力度与微时序，否则和 loop 一样死。第四，风格靠 EZX 扩展，核心库覆盖有限，每个扩展几 GB，核心库的军鼓与镲片选择比扩展少。第五，授权绑定账户，转售要走厂商的许可转移流程。第六，电鼓的 hi-hat 与边击支持不如高端模块细。第七，它不做鼓替换与音高检测，对齐原始录音要别的工具，别指望它当采样器。第八，界面信息密度高，第一次找功能会慢。

## 替代与搭配

要深度混音与采样替换，换 Superior Drummer 3；要更宽的多风格覆盖，换 Addictive Drums；要电子鼓机，换 Battery 或硬件；只做编程鼓，买 Battery 加采样更自由；要管弦打击乐，另找专用打击乐库。它常与总线压缩、并行失真搭配，把 demo 鼓做出冲击。

## What it lets you finish

A demo gets a presentable drum part without writing every hit. The groove browser finds patterns by style and tempo, dragged in and edited. Tap2Find takes a tapped rhythm and returns close matches. Bandmate suggests kits from a reference track. Song Track lays out verses, choruses and fills. It can also serve as a live drum machine. Electronic drums can play parts live, and an ad can get a usable drum track within half an hour. Drum stems can be exported for mixing.

## How it works

Sampled drums with built-in processing: each kit arrives with room, compression and EQ already shaping a finished sound. The core library is around fifteen gigabytes and usually loads in seconds; samples are layered by drum and velocity. MIDI grooves are organised by style, meter and complexity and can be transposed to the project, and a dragged groove keeps its velocities and ornaments. The mix preset can be swapped as a whole or per drum. Articulations use keyswitches, open hi-hat follows CC4, and electronic kits can be mapped. Multiple outputs separate kick, snare and room.

## Limits and traps

The sound is already mixed: replacing a snare, adjusting bleed or rebuilding the room requires Superior Drummer 3. Microphone and leakage control are limited, which shows in detailed drum mixing. Grooves used as-is sound generic, so velocity and microtiming need editing or the part feels like a loop. Styles depend on EZX expansions, the core library is limited, core snare and cymbal choices are fewer than in the expansions, and each expansion takes a few gigabytes. Authorisation is account-bound, and resale must go through the vendor's transfer process. Electronic hi-hat and rim response are less detailed than on high-end modules. It does no drum replacement or pitch detection, so aligning an original recording needs another tool, and it should not be treated as a sampler. The interface is dense, which slows a first visit.

## Alternatives and pairings

For deep mixing and sample replacement, Superior Drummer 3; for broader multi-style coverage, Addictive Drums; for electronic drum machines, Battery or hardware; for pure programming, Battery with your own samples is freer; for orchestral percussion, a dedicated percussion library. It pairs with bus compression and parallel distortion to give demo drums impact.

## First three things to learn

Learn groove search and drag-and-drop, multiple outputs, and velocity with microtiming. Take one eight-bar song through the whole chain, then read the site's drum kit, velocity, and microtiming and groove entries.
